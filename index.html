Our Story

Three Guys, One Dream

Born from a shared passion for good food and great company, Three Guys Cafe & Lounge was built on one simple belief: that every meal should feel like a celebration. Tucked away on Ghaziabad's G.T. Road, we've created a space where families dine, friends laugh, and couples find their favourite corner table.

From sizzling North Indian classics to crowd-pleasing Chinese, wood-fired pizzas to towering burgers — our kitchen is a melting pot of flavours, served fresh and with heart every single day.
